EuroNails

A nail salon on the second floor at 1901 Post Road in Fairfield, Connecticut. Manicures, pedicures, gel and Russian gel, nail art, and repairs, by appointment.

Where to find us

1901 Post Rd, 2nd Floor
Fairfield, CT 06824

We are set back behind the row of shops that front Post Road, so you cannot see us from the street. Driving along Post Road, the entrance to our parking lot is immediately to the left of the podiatrist's office. The staircase up to the second floor is exterior, across from Kitchen Melissa, with a board at the foot of it reading 1901 Post Road. There is no elevator, and there is no sign for the salon — not outside, not at the stairs, and not on the door at the top.

Parking is free, with a larger lot at the back and a few marked spaces in front, including accessible spaces.

Booking by text

If you would like to book, we can text you a link to our online booking page. We only ever do this when you ask us to, or agree to it, during a call or a text conversation that you started.

On a call, if you ask for the link — or we offer and you say yes — we will tell you it is one message from EuroNails, that message and data rates may apply, and that you can reply STOP at any time to opt out. Then we ask for your mobile number, read it back to you, and wait for you to confirm it before anything is sent. If you would rather not, we do not send it.

By text, if you text us first, your message starts the conversation and we reply in it.

We do not send marketing, promotions, bulk messages, or any proactive outreach, and we never message a number that has not contacted us first. Typically this is one message per conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
