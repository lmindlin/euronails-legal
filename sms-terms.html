EuroNails — Text Message Terms and Conditions

Effective 1 August 2026. Updated 8 October 2026.

Program name

EuroNails Booking Link Messages, and EuroNails Staff Appointment Notifications.

What the program does

For customers. When you call or text EuroNails, we may reply by text and, at your request or with your agreement, send you a link to our online booking page. Messages are sent only in direct response to a call or text that you started. We do not send marketing or promotional messages under this program, and we do not message a customer's number that has not contacted us.

For salon staff. The salon owner and EuroNails nail technicians can receive texts about the salon's appointments: one when an appointment is booked, changed or cancelled, and one each morning with that day's schedule. The owner receives notices about all appointments; each technician receives notices only about their own. These texts go only to staff who have agreed in writing to receive them, at the mobile number they gave the salon. They contain the client's first name, the services, the technician, the time and the length of the appointment, and never a client's phone number. Customers never receive these messages.

Staff appointment notifications are sent from (475) 242-4053, a number used only for these texts. It does not reply to messages; to reach the salon, call or text (475) 445-3212.

The first text each staff member receives is a welcome message. It says what the alerts are, that their frequency varies with bookings, that message and data rates may apply, and how to get help (reply HELP) or stop (reply STOP). Later alerts do not repeat these instructions; STOP works at any time.

Message frequency

Customers: message frequency varies and depends entirely on your interaction with us. Typically this is one message per conversation. There is no recurring or scheduled messaging to customers.

Staff: message frequency varies with the number of bookings: one message for each booking, change or cancellation, and one schedule on each day with appointments.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. EuroNails does not charge for these messages; your mobile carrier's standard rates for text and data apply. Contact your carrier for details of your plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL and UNSUBSCRIBE work too, and so does a plain request such as "please stop texting me". You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent to that number unless you contact us again to opt back in (reply START).

Staff can also opt out by telling the owner by phone, email or in person. The opt-out is recorded straight away and no further staff texts are sent. The salon keeps a record of when each staff member agreed to these texts and of every opt-out and opt-in. ## How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, call us at (475) 445-3212, or email euronailsct@gmail.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed, and messages may be filtered or delayed by your carrier or device.

Supported carriers

Messages are supported on major US carriers. Carrier support may change without notice.

Privacy

Information collected through this program is handled as described in our Privacy Policy. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.
