EuroNails — Text Message Terms and Conditions

Effective 1 August 2026

Program name

EuroNails Booking Link Messages.

What the program does

When you call or text EuroNails, we may reply by text and, at your request or with your agreement, send you a link to our online booking page. Messages are sent only in direct response to a call or text that you started. We do not send marketing or promotional messages under this program, and we do not message numbers that have not contacted us.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends entirely on your interaction with us. Typically this is one message per conversation. There is no recurring or scheduled messaging.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. EuroNails does not charge for these messages; your mobile carrier's standard rates for text and data apply. Contact your carrier for details of your plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent to that number unless you contact us again to opt back in.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, call us at (203) 220-3232, or email euronailsct@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed, and messages may be filtered or delayed by your carrier or device.

Supported carriers

Messages are supported on major US carriers. Carrier support may change without notice.

Privacy

Information collected through this program is handled as described in our Privacy Policy. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.
